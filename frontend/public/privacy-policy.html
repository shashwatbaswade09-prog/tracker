Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 June 2026

Operator: AKOLE MEDIA PRIVATE LIMITED (“Nexus Media”, “we”, “us”, “our”)

Contact: support@thenexusmedia.org

This policy explains what personal data Nexus Media collects, why we collect it, how we share it, and what control you have over it. It applies to everyone who uses the Nexus Media web application at https://app.thenexusmedia.org and any connected services.

1. Who we are

Nexus Media is a clipping-agency operations platform. Brands (“clients”) commission short-form video clips for marketing campaigns; freelancers (“clippers”) submit clips and earn payment based on engagement. We provide the workflow, attribution, and stats tracking; we do not own the underlying social media accounts or content.

2. Information we collect

2a. Information you give us directly

2b. Information we receive from social platforms when you connect an account

When you connect a Facebook Page, Instagram Business/Creator account, TikTok account, or YouTube channel via OAuth, we receive and store:

  • An OAuth access token and (where issued) a refresh token, encrypted at rest with AES-256
  • Your platform account ID and public username/handle on that platform
  • The OAuth scopes you granted (e.g. read_insights, video.list)
  • Token expiry timestamp

We use these tokens only to fetch publicly-visible engagement metrics (views, likes, comments, shares, country breakdown) for the specific clips you have submitted to Nexus campaigns. We do not post on your behalf, message your contacts, read your inbox, access friends or follower lists, or modify your account in any way.

2c. Information generated by your use of Nexus

  • Submission stats fetched from each connected platform’s API: views, likes, comments, shares
  • Country-level view breakdowns (where available)
  • Daily per-campaign clip counts (for payment computation)
  • Payment records: amount, currency, admin notes, timestamps
  • Credential access logs — when an admin or a clipper reveals a shared platform credential, we record the user ID, the credential accessed, the IP address, and the timestamp for security auditing

2d. Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, and approximate location (for rate limiting and security)
  • Page-level usage events via PostHog (no personally identifying content is sent — only anonymous page views and feature interactions)

We do not collect: government ID, payment card numbers (Nexus does not process money — payments are made out-of-band), biometric data, precise GPS location, contact lists, or messages.

3. How we use your information

We use your information solely to:

  • Operate the Nexus Media platform (sign-in, role-based access, OTP verification)
  • Display your campaign-level performance stats and country demographics
  • Verify that clips you submit actually belong to the social account you connected
  • Calculate clip counts and surface payment status to admins
  • Send transactional email (sign-up OTP, password reset OTP, payment notifications, waitlist approval, daily admin reports)
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorised access
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data to train AI models. We do not show advertisements.

6. Your rights

Subject to verification of your identity, you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data via your profile settings
  • Delete your account and the personal data associated with it (some records, such as payments, will be retained for the periods listed above to satisfy legal obligations)
  • Disconnect any connected social account at any time, which immediately revokes our access to that platform’s APIs for your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for any optional processing
  • Object to processing for legitimate-interest reasons

To exercise any of these rights, email support@thenexusmedia.org from the address on your Nexus account. We respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Security

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (work factor 12); we never store plaintext passwords
  • All connected-account credentials (OAuth tokens, shared platform passwords) are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM
  • Every shared-credential reveal is logged with user ID, IP address, and timestamp
  • HTTPS is enforced on all traffic between your browser and our servers
  • Database access is gated behind a connection pooler with role-scoped permissions

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a personal-data breach, we will notify affected users and the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours as required by applicable law.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only essential cookies:

  • A session cookie to keep you signed in
  • A CSRF token cookie to prevent cross-site request forgery
  • A PostHog session identifier (pseudonymous, no personal data)

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. No banner is shown because we use only strictly-necessary cookies, which do not require consent under GDPR.

9. Children

Nexus Media is not directed to and may not be used by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has registered, email us and we will delete the account.

10. International transfers

Your data is stored on servers operated by Supabase (currently in ap-south-1 / Mumbai), Upstash, and our hosting provider. By using Nexus Media you consent to your data being transferred to and processed in those jurisdictions, which may differ from your country of residence.
